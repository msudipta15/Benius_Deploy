BENIUS — EXAM CONTROLLER PROMOTION VERDICT CONSISTENCY AUDIT

MODE: STRICT READ-ONLY — NO IMPLEMENTATION

PRIORITY: HIGH

OBJECTIVE:

Audit whether Teacher recommendations, Principal final promotion/retention decisions, and executed promotion outcomes are consistently reflected across the Principal Exam Controller, Principal Analytics, Teacher Examination Results, and Student Examination Results.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:

BENIUS is a multi-tenant school management application.

Every Exam Controller operation and historical result must be strictly scoped to:

1. Authenticated school_id.
2. Selected Academic Session ID.
3. Correct student and applicable examination context.

Do not mix decisions from different schools or Academic Sessions.

This appears inconsistent.

First determine whether these records refer to the same student, Academic Session, examination context, and execution.

- Teacher recommendation storage and retrieval.
- Principal promotion/retention override storage.

- Execution history and decision linkage.
- Final promotion/retention verdict resolution.
- Destination class and section.
- Archived-session historical display.

Determine why the screenshot appears to show an applied Retain override alongside Principal Final: Promoted.

AUDIT 3 — TEACHER EXAMINATION RESULTS

Inspect the Teacher Portal Examination → Results module.

Determine:

AUDIT 4 — STUDENT EXAMINATION RESULTS

Inspect Student Portal → Examination → Results.

Determine:

- Whether the correct Academic Session and Student enrollment are used.

IMPORTANT:
Do not expose unpublished or internal decisions to Students merely to make portal displays consistent.
